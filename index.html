Privacy Policy

Card Counter  ·  Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Card Counter iOS app ("the App") handles your information. The short version: it does not collect anything. The App has no networking code of any kind — it cannot send data anywhere, because it never makes a network request.

1. Information We Collect

None. The App collects no personal information whatsoever. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no server. We never see how you use the App, or that you use it at all.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App saves two things, both locally, and neither ever leaves your device except as part of a standard encrypted iPhone backup, which is controlled entirely by your own Apple account settings:

  • Your training history — the drills you have answered, whether each was correct, and running accuracy totals. Stored as a JSON file in the App's private Application Support directory, readable only by the App.
  • Your preferences — deck count, dealing speed, table rules and display options. Stored locally using iOS UserDefaults.

Deleting the App removes both. You can also clear your training history at any time from the Progress screen.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage data.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, or track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, or contacts.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data — we do not have any of it to share.

4. Third-Party Services

None. The App contains no third-party SDKs, frameworks, or analytics libraries. It is built entirely on Apple's own frameworks and makes no outbound connections.

5. Children's Privacy

The App simulates a casino card game for training purposes and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Common Questions

What is the running count?
The Hi-Lo total of every card you have seen this shoe: 2–6 count as +1, 7–9 as 0, and 10, J, Q, K, A as −1. Over a full deck the tags cancel out to zero.

Why is the true count a whole number?
Because that is how it is used at a table. You judge the discard tray to about half a deck, divide the running count by the decks remaining, and round. The App shows you the deck estimate it is using, so the answer is always one you could have worked out yourself.

Why did my count not finish at zero?
Cards can leave the shoe unseen — a burn card, or a hole card the dealer never turned over. The App only ever grades you on cards you could actually have seen, so an honest count may end slightly off zero. The quiz tells you how many cards went unseen.

Why does removing tens change the count?
Hi-Lo is balanced only with full decks. Remove all the tens and the tags no longer cancel, so a perfect count finishes at +24 rather than 0 in a six-deck shoe. The App shows this as the shoe's imbalance and grades the true count against the adjusted figure, which is the one tied to your edge.

How do the simulated players decide what to do?
Every seat plays perfect basic strategy for the rules you have set — hit, stand, double, split and surrender all follow the chart for your deck count, soft-17 rule and doubling restrictions.

Is my data private?
Yes. The App has no networking code at all, so nothing can leave your device. See the Privacy Policy above.
